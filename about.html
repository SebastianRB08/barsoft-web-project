02 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

A practical idea for busy hospitality teams.

Barsoft is a website about a simple management concept for bars, cafés, and restaurants.

✳

What is the project about?

Barsoft brings everyday information—sales, stock, and team tasks—into one clear place. The website presents a visual example of how a digital management tool could support a hospitality business.

◎

What is its purpose?

The purpose of this project is to show how a simple digital tool can make daily work easier to follow. It helps visitors understand the idea through clear information and easy navigation.

01

TARGET USERS

Who can use this idea?

Small business owners and their teams can use a shared overview of sales, inventory, and daily tasks. Barsoft is a student project concept, not a finished point-of-sale service.
