02 / ABOUT THE PROJECT

A practical idea for busy hospitality teams.

Barsoft is a website about a simple management concept for bars, cafés, and restaurants. It brings everyday information—sales, stock, and team tasks—into one clear place.

The purpose of this project is to show how a digital tool can make daily work easier to follow. Its target users are small business owners and their teams. Barsoft is a student project concept, not a finished point-of-sale service.
